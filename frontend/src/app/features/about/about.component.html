Our Mission

Founded by orthopedic surgeons, physical therapists, and bio-mechanical engineers, PhysioProgress is on a mission to eliminate blind spots in home rehabilitation. We replace static paper handouts with intelligent, sensor-grade kinematic bio-feedback.

Traditional physical therapy rehabilitation often relies on patient compliance without real-time oversight. Our platform bridges the gap between in-clinic sessions and home recovery through real-time kinematic tracking, adaptive exercise protocols, and continuous clinical oversight—all in one unified ecosystem.
